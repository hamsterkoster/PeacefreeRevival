# 6. Settings on the title and act screens, volume sliders (medium)
Goal: "Settings should be accessible from the main page / act selection page. The volumes should also be a slider not just on/off."
- Problem: the only settings panel is the options menu inside a mission (`OptionsInterfacer`; it needs `pgame.map` for Difficulty, Exit Game and closing). Music and SFX are on/off only (`SoundLibrary.ENABLE_SFX`, `bEnabledBGM`; music plays at a fixed 0.35 through `SoundLibrary.BGM_VOLUME`, sound effects at full volume: `playSFX` passes no volume); the title and campaign screens have just two on/off icons each, top left.
- Read first: `OptionsInterfacer.as` (our copy in `src/`, with "Unlock All Acts"), `SoundLibrary.as` (`playSFX`, `playBGM`, `BGM_VOLUME`), `TitleScreen.as` and `CampaignSelectorScreen.as` (`sfxButton`, `bgmButton`, `toggleBGM`, `toggleSFX`; the campaign top bar), `PeacefreeGame.saveGameData` (what the save holds).
- Approach: a Settings button on both screens opening the same panel, with mission-only rows hidden outside a mission; a slider (bar plus draggable knob, Starling touch events; neither Starling 1.4.1 nor the game has one) for music and SFX volume, built inside `OptionsInterfacer` because `build.py` (ffdec `-replace`) can only replace existing classes; volumes saved in a `settings` save through `FileLibrary`, so all builds share them (to-do 5).
- Answers (the user took the suggestions, 2026-10-31):
  - Sliders: 0-100% in 5% steps, 100% = full volume; music starts at 35% (the game's own level), SFX at 100%.
  - Button: a "Settings" text button on both screens, first in the top left, with the two sound icons moved to its right (the user's change while testing v08; first planned after the icons).
  - The old icons stay as quick mute/unmute; the panel shows "Off" beside a muted slider, and dragging it unmutes.
  - Volumes in their own `settings` save, read at start by the sound code; the mute flags stay in the game's save, so `PeacefreeGame` isn't patched.
  - Outside a mission the panel shows Music, SFX, Unlock All Acts and a Back button; Difficulty and Exit Game are hidden. Unlocking on the campaign screen reloads it (a short fade) so the new acts show.
  - Two steps, A and B below.
- Test: the headless checks pass, plus a new `check.py --settings`; in the projector, the panel opens from the title, act and mission screens, the sliders change the volume while you listen, and the volumes are still set after a restart.
