# Changelog

What changed for players in each release of Peacefree Revival, the unofficial fan patch of Peacefree: Tactical Warfare. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/); the version is the one on the title screen ("Revival 1.0"). Compared with the original game, version 1.08 from Kongregate.

## [Unreleased]
The first release, 1.0; it gets its date when it's published.

### Added
- A Windows app that runs the game without Flash Player, as an installer (for your user only, no admin prompt; a Start menu entry and a desktop shortcut) or a zip, with a readme that the installer shows first.
- Premium is unlocked, since it can no longer be bought: Cross Zap, the Shock and Nox bombs, the extra watchtower upgrades (Blaster, Noxxer, Zap sentry), the bonus resources at the start of a mission, and Act 11, the Endless Boss Run.
- 10 Shock and 10 Nox bombs at the start of every game; the shop gives 10 more of each for free.
- A Settings button at the top left of the title and campaign screens opens the options panel there too.
- Music and sound effect volume sliders in the options panel, saved between games.
- A Start Speed setting (1x, 4x or 8x) for the speed new acts start at; Act 1, the tutorial, keeps 4x.
- A Next button in a mission's options panel skips to the next song.
- A Fullscreen button in the options panel; Esc leaves fullscreen.
- A Controls page in the options panel lists every key.
- "Unlock All Acts" in the options panel opens every act at once. It can't be undone.
- The P key shows or hides the frame rate.
- The title screen shows the release version: "Revival 1.0, unofficial fan patch".

### Changed
- The game makes no network connections: its link to Kongregate is replaced by an offline stand-in.
- Muting the music pauses the song, and unmuting continues it.
- The game grows and shrinks with its window, keeping its shape with black bars.
- In a mission, the keys do nothing while the options panel is open.
- The shop says the bombs are free.
- The saves have names of their own (`peacefree-game` and so on), so they don't mix with other Flash games' saves.

### Fixed
- Resizing the window no longer stops the game.
