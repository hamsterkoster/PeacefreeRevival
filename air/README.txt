Peacefree Revival
=================
Version {version}

Peacefree: Tactical Warfare, the Flash game by Adventure Cat, LLC, as a Windows app that runs
without Flash Player. This is an unofficial fan patch: the premium features are unlocked, the
game makes no network connections, and it has quality-of-life changes but no gameplay changes.
It isn't made or endorsed by Adventure Cat, LLC, Helgravis or Kongregate, and it comes down if
the developer asks.

The project, its other downloads and what changed:
https://github.com/hamsterkoster/PeacefreeRevival


Start
-----
- Installed: Peacefree Revival in the Start menu, or its desktop shortcut.
- From the zip: unzip it and open "Peacefree Revival.exe" in the "Peacefree Revival" folder.
- The first time, Windows may say "Windows protected your PC", because the app isn't
  code-signed: click "More info", then "Run anyway".
- A splash screen shows for about 2 seconds first (the free AIR SDK's).


Good to know
------------
- The Settings button at the top left of the title and campaign screens opens the options:
  music and sound volume, the speed new acts start at, Fullscreen and the list of keys
  (Controls). In a mission, the game's own options button opens the same panel.
- "Unlock All Acts" in the options opens every act at once. It can't be undone.
- P shows or hides the frame rate.
- Your progress is saved in this folder (paste it into File Explorer's address bar):
  %APPDATA%\io.github.hamsterkoster.PeacefreeRevival\Local Store
  Uninstalling keeps it. Copy that folder to back it up. The Flash Player projector and
  Ruffle keep their own saves, apart from this app's.


Uninstall
---------
- Installed: Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps > Peacefree Revival > Uninstall.
- From the zip: delete the folder.


Privacy
-------
Peacefree Revival collects no data and makes no network connections. The only links are the
title screen's Facebook and Twitter buttons, which open the developer's pages in your browser
when you click them. Privacy policy:
https://github.com/hamsterkoster/PeacefreeRevival#privacy
